How this works

Each member's basin-mean week-2 temperature and precipitation is ranked within its own model's reforecast climate (GEFSv12 2000-2019, ECMWF 2006-2025, starts within 21 days of the date). The share of members in the tail, GEFS and ECMWF half each, is turned into odds with a logistic fit to the 2000-2025 hindcast for the season. Nov-Feb hindcast at the 15% alert level: about 2.5-5 alerts a winter per basin, right 24-29% of the time against a 9-13% base rate, catching about half to two-thirds of events. Truth: PRISM Climate Group, Oregon State University (basin means). Forecast data: NOAA GEFS via dynamical.org; ECMWF open data, © ECMWF, CC BY 4.0 (reduced here to basin statistics).
