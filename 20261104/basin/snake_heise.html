Members, day by day

Running basin-mean precipitation total (inches) from the start, one row per member, wettest first by the end of week 2. Week 2 is shaded in the header. Flags mark members whose week 2 is in an alarm tail of their own model's climate, and their week-2 cells are outlined: WD warm-dry, D dry, CW cold-wet. Wk-2 P is the member's days 8-14 precipitation (inches) and its percentile in its own model's climate, in the flag colour when flagged; Wk-2 T is its week-2 temperature percentile. Flags depend on week 2 only. ECMWF members stop at day 15, so their Total covers 15 days, not 21.
