How this works

Each member's subregion-mean week-2 temperature and precipitation is ranked within its own model's reforecast climate, and the share of members in the tail (GEFS and ECMWF half each) is turned into odds with one logistic fit per signal and season, pooled over all subregions. Forecast fields are averaged onto 1.5 degree boxes before the subregion mean, to match the reforecast archive. Hindcast (GEFS and ECMWF reforecasts against CPC gauge precipitation and station temperature): detection is best for cold-wet and warm-dry weeks and weakest for dry-only weeks, and warm-dry skill is lower in the northern interior than in California and the Southwest. "Past detection skill" is the ROC area for that subregion and season (0.5 is no skill, 1 is perfect); it rests on about 25 events per subregion, so treat it as rough. Subregion boundaries: USGS Watershed Boundary Dataset. Forecast data: NOAA GEFS via dynamical.org; ECMWF open data, © ECMWF, CC BY 4.0. Truth for the hindcast: NOAA CPC unified gauge analysis.
