Experimental: these basins open no alerts. Odds are calibrated against PRISM basin means, pooled over the water-supply HUC8s. Each member's week-2 temperature and precipitation is ranked within its own model's reforecast climate; flags mark members in an alarm tail (WD warm-dry, D dry, WW warm-wet, CW cold-wet). The table shows the running basin-mean precipitation total (inches) from the start, wettest member first by the end of week 2. ECMWF members stop at day 15. Past skill is the ROC area in past forecasts for this basin and season: 0.5 is none, 1 is perfect, and it rests on about 25 events.
